Hi, I'm Rohan!

My love for computers started with video games. My earliest gaming memory was playing Midtown Madness. Since then, I've played on a variety of platforms, including the Game Boy Advance SP, Nintendo DS, PSP, PS2, and PS3. Eventually, I built my first PC.

Building my first PC sparked a curiosity about how software worked, eventually leading me to programming. It's been my favourite hobby ever since.

Once I started playing on PC, I found myself drawn to competitive games like Counter-Strike and Rocket League. I've logged over 1,200 hours in each and reached Champion 3 in Rocket League, a milestone I'm still proud of.

I grew up with a passion for two things: computers and space. I've always loved stargazing, and one day I'd like to own a telescope to photograph planets, galaxies, and other celestial objects.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me reading a book, listening to music, or building something new.
